Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Bhandalkar Mess (proprietor: Omkar Sunil Bhandalkar) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email address and password (stored in protected form).
  • Service records: your tiffin entries, bills and payment history.
  • Notification preference: if you allow browser notifications, the information needed to send them to your device.

How we use it

  • To run your account, record tiffins, prepare your bill and show your payment history.
  • To send you bill reminders, payment confirmations and one-time passwords (OTP) by email or notification.
  • To contact you about your order or a payment.

Payments

Online payments are processed by a third-party payment gateway. Your card, UPI and bank details are entered on the gateway’s secure page and are not stored by us. We only receive the result of the payment and its reference.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers needed to run the website (such as hosting, email delivery and the payment gateway), or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account information by contacting us. Some billing records may need to be kept as required by law.
