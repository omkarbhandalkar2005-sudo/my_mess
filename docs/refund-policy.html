Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Cancelling a tiffin

You can cancel a day’s tiffin or extra items by informing us before the food is prepared. Cancelled items are not charged. Items that have already been prepared or taken are charged at the listed price.

What you pay for

You are charged only for tiffins and items recorded against your account. If you notice a wrong or duplicate entry, tell us and we will correct it and adjust your bill.

Refunds for online payments

A refund is made when an online payment was charged twice, charged in excess, or charged more than your pending bill. After we verify the payment, the refund is sent to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) within 5–7 working days.

Failed payments

If money was deducted from your account but the payment failed or does not show against your bill, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank within 5–7 working days. If it is not, contact us with the payment details and we will help.

Payments already adjusted

Once an online payment has been adjusted against a valid bill for food that was served, it is not refundable.

How to request a refund

Email omkarbhandalkar2005@gmail.com or call +91 9960903354. Please share your name, the date and amount of the payment, and the payment ID if you have it.
